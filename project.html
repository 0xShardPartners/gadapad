Ondol Protocol builds real world asset yield vaults for the GIWA ecosystem. Each vault is collateralized by tokenized short term credit instruments, including trade receivables and invoice financing sourced from vetted originators, and pays out on-chain in a GIWA native stablecoin as the underlying credit performs rather than as a fixed APY.

Originators are onboarded through a credit committee that reviews payment history and collateral quality before any receivable is tokenized. Vault performance, repayment schedules, and default events are all readable on-chain, so LPs are not relying on a quarterly PDF to know what they hold.

The team has run two testnet cohorts with institutional LPs since Q1, processing over ₩54,000,000,000 in simulated vault volume before bringing the product to a public sale.

Highlights

Participant governed treasury

Funds raised in this sale settle into a treasury contract scoped to Ondol Protocol alone. Reaching the hardcap does not unlock it. To spend anything the team files a request with a stated purpose, and every wallet that contributed to this raise can vote, weighted by what it put in. No quorum, no release.
