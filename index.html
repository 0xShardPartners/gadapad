Participant governed treasury

Raising the money is the easy part. Spending it is where teams disappear.

On most launchpads a cleared sale sends funds straight to a wallet the team controls outright. From that moment the people who funded the raise have no mechanism left. They can only watch.

GADAPAD treats that gap as the actual point of failure. Funds settle into a treasury contract scoped to one launch, and hitting the hardcap does not unlock it. To spend anything the team files a request with a stated purpose, and the contributors who funded that launch vote on it, weighted by what they put in. No quorum, no release.
